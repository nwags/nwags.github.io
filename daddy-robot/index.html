DADDY ROBOT · Featherweight first. Championship ambitions.

We’re aiming high.
We’re keeping the robot low.

A modular robot-combat program bringing precision manufacturing, advanced materials, airflow, electromagnetics and controls together in a design built to evolve.

Our first competition-design target is a 30 lb featherweight. Each experiment teaches us what to carry into the next machine.

Build · Test · Improve

One modular platform. Many ways forward.

The central body, independent outer rotating ring, mobility modules and control systems are intended to be serviceable and replaceable. That means faster repairs, more focused experiments, and the freedom to compare different concepts.

Instead of betting everything on an untested final assembly, we aim to make incremental, evidence-driven progress.

Engineered for iteration

  • Interchangeable external ring configurations
  • Compact, protected central structure
  • Modular mobility and electrical subsystems
  • Prototypes that reveal manufacturability, performance and reliability

Concepts shown here are design studies; future builds and competition entry depend on engineering validation and event rules.

The road ahead

Start small. Learn fast. Scale deliberately.

Progressive development

30 lb Featherweight: initial competition-design target, including possible NHRL participation.

120–150 lb middleweight-scale: longer-term development goal; venue to be determined.

Up to 250 lb heavyweight: eventual ambition, subject to applicable competition requirements.

Every stage is informed by testing and engineering results.
