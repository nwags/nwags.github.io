Initial release scope

Overview and Financing explore 33 prepared division/scenario cases, with comparison against the same division's Central reference. An arithmetic worksheet checks reconciliation; a pass is not financial-policy feasibility or approval. This edition does not perform arbitrary new what-if calculation or live optimization.

Evidence and limitations

The evidence cutoff is November 7, 2026. Bill examples use 5,000 billable gallons per month equivalent, a 5/8-inch meter and one equivalent dwelling unit where applicable; suburban wastewater uses common rate 1. This is not a live ERP connection. Expanded evidence views and independent comparator selection are later additions, not current features.
