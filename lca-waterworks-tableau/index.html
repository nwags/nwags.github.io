Available in this edition

Overview and Financing cover 33 prepared division/scenario cases, including bill and cash paths, annual sources and uses, and capital versus identified funding. The workbook’s all-case arithmetic audit passed in Desktop. Arithmetic reconciliation is not financial-policy feasibility or approval.

What the controls do

Choose a division and a prepared scenario. The comparison is fixed to that division’s Central reference. This edition does not recalculate arbitrary new assumptions or run a live optimizer. The separate JavaScript application retains those broader planning controls.

Additional views are still planned

This initial edition has fewer pages than the Power BI report. Selectable scenario comparisons, customer-bill components, capital reconciliation, historical/customer trends, scenario assumptions, rules, glossary and expanded audit/integration views remain on the completion plan. They are not hidden features of this embed.

Evidence and illustrated account

Evidence cutoff: November 7, 2026. Bills use 5,000 billable gallons per month equivalent, a 5/8-inch meter and one equivalent dwelling unit where applicable; suburban wastewater uses common rate 1.
