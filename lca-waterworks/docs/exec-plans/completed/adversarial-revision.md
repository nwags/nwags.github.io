## Acceptance criteria

1. Separate executable ledgers for all three reporting divisions. No silent interfund balancing.
2. All five annual published capital cash schedules retained. Original published cash and derived scenario cash are distinguished; category or arithmetic discrepancies are visible.
3. Resolve agreement scope where primary evidence permits. Explicit assumptions enable remaining scenario controls; no assumed legal term is labeled verified.
4. Actual fixed, usage, block and City add-on charges drive illustrative bills. A rate freeze reproduces the prior tariff. Revenue mix assumptions do not change when household usage changes.
5. Explicitly named bill comparator; clear zero-difference wording; comparison includes cash, coverage and financing as well as bills.
6. Proposed 2027 tariffs, approved budget and published drivers anchor the planning default. Historical evidence informs named sensitivity scenarios without fabricated probabilities.
7. Customer counts/units, volume and revenue by class retain their distinct scope, period and units.
8. A separate model page diagrams cash flows and explains equations, calibration, assumptions and limits.
9. Versioned exports/import validation, transparent bounded optimization, synthetic-only connector fixtures, current manual and user guide.
10. Independent second review, meaningful financial tests, observed browser checks and verified publication.

## Workers and boundaries

- Financial extraction: workhorse model, medium reasoning; source tables, accounting bridge, historical and customer evidence.
- Tariff/agreement interpretation: workhorse model, high reasoning; exact components, legal scope and explicit assumption contract.
- Adversarial reviewer: frontier model, high reasoning; independent semantic/numerical/UX challenge and second review.
- Controller: architecture, integration, implementation, visual QA and publication. Workers do not publish or contact stakeholders.

Bounded retrieval passes and concrete handoffs keep the three parallel streams separable. Routine extraction does not use maximum effort. Escalation is reserved for accounting contradictions, unresolved agreement interpretation or failing invariants.

## Findings changing the implementation

- Budget revenue totals include grants in two divisions; rate scaling and separate grant lines must avoid double counting.
- The original cash operating expense combined operating and other capital payments. Their growth drivers must be separated.
- City published CIP cash balances do not all reconcile to its condensed annual flow rows. Preserve the reported balances and show the arithmetic difference rather than insert a hidden balancing flow.
- Component-level tariff changes are necessary to make a rate freeze and household comparisons exact.
