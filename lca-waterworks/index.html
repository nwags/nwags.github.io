Five-year borrowing, grants and remaining cash bridge · USD

Zero additional debt keeps planned borrowing. The control changes only additional draws. For the full-period cash identity, capital spending equals borrowing + grants + capital receipts + cash generated + investment conversions + opening less ending cash. Cash generated here deducts operating, lease, all debt service and other capital payments. A negative reserve draw means reserves build.

Each year pairs spending with identified borrowing, grants and capital receipts. This compares aggregate timing; it does not earmark a receipt to a project or certify approval or eligibility. Applicability is resolved where the public record supports it. Remaining missing terms become explicit planning parameters; they do not lock the controls. Numerical feasibility and permission to adopt rates are separate conclusions.

City core-rate ceiling

The original lease specifies a nonnegative annual change based on the prior June annual change in CPI-U for the Northeast, not seasonally adjusted, plus a 2.5-point margin through 2032. Municipal agreements and permitted add-ons have separate treatment. The model checks a core-growth proxy and exposes an explicit amendment assumption for analysis.

Published volume rates include embedded pass-throughs that are not separately decomposed here, so this is a planning check rather than a certified invoice audit. The 2020 council approval and published implementation history were verified; the reviewed execution-copy file has blank signature fields.
