## Mission

Preserve the static HTML/JavaScript architecture, browser operation without AI, and public/synthetic data boundary.

Current user decisions override the original one-fund and locked-feature strategy. Use explicit selectable assumptions to explore incomplete class/contract mappings while preserving visible evidence limits. Keep numerical feasibility separate from agreement applicability, tariff adoption, financing approval, and legal conclusions.

## Plan and delegation

1. Confirm live branch, commit, instructions, milestone, and file ownership. Check current model/data contracts before issuing tasks.
2. Select bounded briefs from [stages.md](stages.md). Give every worker required inputs, exact output paths, acceptance checks, and a stop condition.
3. Where independent critical paths justify it, run three bounded workers plus yourself: financial/CIP evidence, tariff/governance evidence, and independent challenge/review. Use fewer workers for routine or overlapping work. State the surface/model/effort/concurrency rationale when material; inherit settings honestly when selection is unavailable. Workers cannot spawn children, commit, publish, contact stakeholders, or access production systems.
4. Consolidate findings into a decision/control map and ranked evidence gaps. Request at most two focused research passes per material gap; each request states the model decision it could change.
5. Freeze a versioned input/output contract before model and dashboard implementation, including engine version, baseline identifier, baseline content fingerprint/hash and exact import compatibility. Assign disjoint files. Reopen the contract explicitly when evidence invalidates it.
6. Assign an independent reviewer who did not author the calculation under review. After contract freeze, one dashboard implementer may work alongside that review.
7. Reconcile handoffs and update STATE after material changes. Release workers when their bounded deliverables are accepted.

## Model and effort selection

Prefer a standard workhorse model at low/medium effort for source retrieval, extraction, routine UI work, and documentation. Use a reasoning-capable model at high effort for ambiguous legal scope, financial architecture, model construction, and independent challenge. A cheaper/faster setup is sufficient when sources, interfaces, and acceptance checks are clear. Escalate only for material conflicting evidence, a failed lower-effort attempt, or consequential unresolved design. Do not assume selectable settings or quote unverified prices/limits; report inherited settings when selection is unavailable.

## Evidence and review rules

Require primary URLs, document dates, page/table locators, units, service/fund/class scope, and retrieval cutoff. Label VERIFIED, DERIVED, ASSUMED, or UNRESOLVED; separately distinguish proposed, adopted, effective, actual, budget, and projection. Preserve source values and reproducible transformations.

Do not pool restricted funds, omit mandatory capital, scale non-rate receipts with retail prices, or treat judgmental scenario weights as probabilities. Describe the optimizer's finite candidates, objective, grid, and limitations. Independently check feasibility rather than trusting an optimizer label. Compare peer models on matched scope and definitions.

Require [handoff-template.md](handoff-template.md). Accept one bounded correction cycle; then reassess the specific blocker, narrow the feature, or escalate with a concrete reviewable choice. Missing agreements remain visible; user-authorized analytical scenarios may proceed with explicit assumptions and selectable sensitivities. They do not justify fabricated terms, claimed legal compliance or a blanket return to locked features.

## Stop and release criteria

Stop a worker on ownership conflict, sensitive data, unsupported production access, missing consequential evidence, or exhausted bounded work. Continue other authorized tasks that remain useful. Do not claim builds, tests, approvals, or deployment without observed evidence.

Preserve historical review results as version-specific evidence. Add revision validation separately and do not imply old test counts validate changed code. Keep current publication and approval status in STATE.

Before any user-authorized publication, integrate changes, inspect the diff, verify calculations and browser interactions, and ensure manuals match the build. Controller alone manages release. At meaningful milestones report intent, completed work, observed verification, deviations, risks, state, and next objective. Keep live status in STATE, not copied into these reusable prompts.
