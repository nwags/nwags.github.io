# Worker handoff template

Copy and complete this template. Keep unavailable values explicitly UNVERIFIED. A handoff is evidence for controller review, not automatic stage acceptance.

- Start/end status: complete, partial, blocked, or needs review:

## Completed work

Describe concrete artifacts and decisions. List changed paths. Explain deviations from the assignment. Do not mark a future design or planned test as implemented.

## Evidence and assumptions

| Claim/input | Label | Value/unit/scope | Source URL and page/table | Document/effective date and status | Transformation or limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fill | VERIFIED / DERIVED / ASSUMED / UNRESOLVED | Fill | Fill | Actual / budget / proposed / adopted / effective / projection | Fill |

For derived values, give original inputs and a reproducible formula. For assumptions, give rationale, range, sensitivity, and who could validate them. Retain conflicting evidence instead of silently choosing a convenient value. Current scope includes all three reporting divisions, without pooling. Current user decisions may supersede first-release scope: record those decisions and allow explicit user-authorized scenario assumptions; never present assumed contract terms as verified authority.

Report commands/tests only if executed and observed. Distinguish numerical feasibility from legal/agreement status and release status. For optimization include objective, candidate family, resolution, tested/feasible counts, and limitations.
