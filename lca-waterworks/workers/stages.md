## 1. Financial evidence

**Setup:** browsing-capable research chat; standard model, medium effort; one of up to three bounded parallel research/review workers, only when separate critical paths justify it.

Collect public LCA budgets, audits, tariffs, CIP schedules, operating measures, and relevant trends. Record exact source locations and original values; distinguish cash from accrual and dollars from thousands. Identify missing volume/class history and assumptions needed for demand priors. Deliver a source ledger, reconciled extraction, and ranked gaps. Do not infer actuals from budgets or fabricate probabilities. Stop after the assigned packet or one requested follow-up pass.

## 2. PA applicability and agreements

**Setup:** browsing-capable reasoning model, high effort; one bounded evidence worker; may run alongside financial extraction and independent challenge.

Determine which authority, statute, regulatory process, municipal agreement, covenant, or stakeholder commitment applies to each modeled service. Prefer official statutes, executed agreements, amendments, and adopted resolutions. Do not assume investor-owned utility rules apply to every public-authority rate. Deliver applicability, effective dates, proposed/adopted distinctions, enforceable numerical rules, and unresolved requirements. Mark interpretations as interpretations; incomplete agreements cannot become invented constraints. Stop at the assigned scope or evidence limit.

## 3. Features and additional research

**Setup:** controller or standard model, medium effort; sequential synthesis.

Translate planning decisions into controls and outputs: bills, revenue, operating costs, capital, financing, cash, coverage, and stress. For each feature identify equation, evidence, assumption, unit, plausible range, and required agreement. Rank gaps by whether they could change a decision. Issue specific follow-up questions, at most two research passes per material gap. Deliver the frozen data/model interface and assumption register, or identify explicitly assumed scenario controls and specific unsupported actions that require separate evidence.

## 4. Financial model and optimization

**Setup:** repository coding chat; reasoning model, high effort; one implementer.

Implement deterministic nominal-dollar calculations against the agreed contract. Preserve fund boundaries, cash rollforward, revenue distinctions, required capital, financing timing, and terminal debt. Explain priors and stress assumptions. Search a disclosed finite rate-path family and show candidate counts, objective/tie-breaks, feasibility, and infeasibility. Include engine version, baseline identifier and content fingerprint/hash in the input/output contract and enforce import compatibility. Deliver equations, reproducible transformations, and focused checks using hand-calculated cases and financial invariants. Do not call a finite candidate winner a global continuous optimum. Stop at the agreed version and checks.

## 5. Independent model and peer review

**Setup:** separate reasoning chat, high effort; reviewer independent of model author.

Reconcile key inputs to sources, hand-check representative years/bills/debt, challenge constraints, and inspect import/export behavior. Compare at least one obtainable public-authority rate model or study; document scope, revenue requirement, allocation, reserves, debt coverage, and affordability differences. Normalize service, usage, units, and financial definitions. If no suitable peer is obtainable within the bounded search, report that gap. Deliver severity-ranked findings and observed evidence, not approval by assertion. Recheck only fixes affecting identified risks.

## 6. Dashboard

**Setup:** repository coding chat; standard model, medium effort; may parallel review after contract freeze.

Build responsive, keyboard-usable controls and understandable charts for the agreed model. Surface baseline/scenario comparisons, assumptions, sources, numerical failures, agreement status, and finite-search limits. Preserve deterministic exports and explicitly synthetic connector behavior. No credentials, customer records, or production requests. Deliver owned UI files and observed browser/interaction checks. Stop at the defined feature list; send model-contract changes to the controller.

## 7. Manual and planning dashboard

Include ERP migration and source-refresh guidance. Deliver manuals and a rehearsal checklist; distinguish performed rehearsal from planned steps.
